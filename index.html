PROJECT: THE CURVES AROUND US

In life, one quantity often changes when another quantity changes. Temperature changes with time, and the price of a taxi changes with distance. Our mission is to investigate real-life curves, collect data and discover mathematical ideas through our own observations.

FIVE INVESTIGATORS · THREE MISSIONS · ONE DISCOVERY

PROJECT GOAL

Discover Through Investigation

We will study curves from real life, perform two experiments, draw graphs, find the greatest and smallest values, and investigate how straight lines intersect curves.

INVESTIGATION RULES

Rules of the Mission

  1. Work in a group of 3 or 4 students, with a time limit of two weeks. Our teacher has approved our group of five.
  2. Do not write mathematical definitions or copy a lesson about functions.
  3. Complete the project tasks in order and use complete sentences when written explanations are required.
  4. Every student must be able to explain all the work.

CLASSROOM DEBATE · ONE QUESTION

The Infinite Hotel Challenge

Imagine a hotel with infinitely many rooms, numbered 1, 2, 3, 4, and so on. Every room is occupied. A new guest arrives and asks for a room. The manager moves the guest in room 1 to room 2, the guest in room 2 to room 3, and so on, moving every guest one room forward. Can the hotel accommodate the new guest without asking anyone to leave?

Take your time. Discuss the consequences of moving every guest. Everyone must think before voting.

THE CORRECT ANSWER: B

Yes. Every guest moves from room n to room n + 1. Room 1 becomes free, and every existing guest still has a room. The surprising idea is that an infinite collection can be rearranged to make space for another member.
