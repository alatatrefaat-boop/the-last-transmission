Mission 03 // The Discovery

THE VERTICAL LINE TEST

We used vertical and horizontal lines to investigate the graphs from Mission 02. We also tested a circle.

PART A — TEST OUR TWO GRAPHS

A vertical line is drawn through one x-value. A horizontal line is drawn through one y-value. Count how many times each line intersects the curve.

PART B — TEST THE FOUR OBSERVATION CURVES

Examine each curve from Mission 01. Imagine drawing vertical lines at different x-values and count the intersections.

PART C — THE CIRCLE EXPERIMENT

The circle below is centered at (0,0) and has a radius of 2 units. Choose a vertical line and see how many times it intersects the circle.

PART D — OUR CONCLUSION

A circle does not represent a single y-value for every x-value. Some vertical lines intersect it twice, while others intersect it once or not at all. Therefore, a complete circle cannot represent a situation in which every input has exactly one output.
